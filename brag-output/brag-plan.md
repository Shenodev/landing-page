# Brag Plan: ShenoDev

## What is this app?

ShenoDev (shenodev.tech) is a premium full-stack web development agency site with a working quote machine: a 5-step project discovery questionnaire, Calendly call scheduling, and confirmation emails — plus transparent fixed EGP pricing and a hidden admin project-upload flow.

## The angle

An agency that sells precision gets a precision launch film: no hype adjectives, just the product doing its thing. The joke-that-isn't-a-joke is the tagline — "Think it, Sheno it." — delivered completely straight, then proven by watching a project go from questionnaire to booked call in seconds.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)

Giant "Think it, Sheno it." typing/slamming in over deep slate with a cyan glow pulse. Four words, instantly readable, weird enough to earn the next 17 seconds.

## Key moments (the middle)

- The real hero headline "High-Performance Web Solutions" rebuilt with its gradient + dual CTAs ("Start Your Project" cursor-clicked).
- The discovery flow simulated live: step cards 01→05 arriving one by one, a file chip attaching, the Calendly pick, then the "Discovery Received!" confirmation stamp.
- The three price cards (10,000 / 25,000 / 45,000 EGP) popping in sequence under the line "Fixed scope. No hidden fees."

## Outro / punchline

"Fixed scope. No hidden fees." holds, then cuts to the ShenoDev logo + shenodev.tech on a final music hit. The punchline is the anti-punchline: an agency bragging about *not* surprising you.

## User flow worth showing

Start Your Project → 5-step discovery questionnaire (info, business, branding, tech, logistics + file attach) → Calendly scheduling → "Thank you — Discovery Received!" confirmation. The centerpiece scene simulates exactly this.

## Tone

- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: premium agency launch film — confident, restrained, quietly funny
- Interpretation: fewer scenes with longer holds; elegance through restraint; humor comes only from delivering "Think it, Sheno it." dead straight

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Share copy (draft)

Think it, Sheno it. ⚡ ShenoDev builds fast, scalable web apps — fixed-scope proposals, no hidden fees. shenodev.tech

## Audio direction

- Role: sleek modern bed with motion-matched accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (60s, ~110 BPM, ende.app) — upbeat business energy that fits an agency launch
- Music treatment: fade in under the hook (0–2s), full presence through the flow/pricing scenes, hard hit + quick fade on the logo outro
- Music cue guidance: preset `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.*` read; ~110 BPM beat grid (~0.55s spacing). Strong cues near 18.01s / 18.55s suit the final logo hit; scene boundaries near ~5.2s, ~10.4s, ~15.8s may snap to nearby beats within ~0.15s. Sequential card reveals should land on every *other* beat at most, then hold the full set.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle — let the cyan glow breathe with music energy in the hook/outro only
- SFX posture: sparse, professional — soft whooshes on scene changes, card pops for sequential reveals, one dry logo hit at the end
- Audio-coupled moments: hook line slam with glow pulse; cursor click on "Start Your Project"; step cards arriving one by one; price cards popping in sequence; confirmation stamp thud
- Restraint rule: music must never fight readability; no SFX under held reading text

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook: "Think it, Sheno it." — 3s

On screen: deep slate background with faint cyan radial glow. The tagline "Think it, Sheno it." slams in large Sora type, cyan glow pulsing once behind it.
Sequential/interaction: none — single slam, then hold for reading (4 words, ~1.5s settled).
Audio intent: music fades in; a soft impact lands with the text.
Audio-coupled idea: text slam + glow pulse on the hit.
Music: upbeat business bed, low under the hook.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Reveal: the hero — 5s

On screen: recreation of the site hero — "Empowering Your Business with High-Performance Web Solutions." (gradient on "High-Performance"), subline, and two CTA buttons. A cursor glides in and clicks "Start Your Project".
Sequential/interaction: yes — headline settles, then subline, then buttons, then the cursor click.
Audio intent: rising confidence; a clean click sound on the CTA press.
Audio-coupled idea: cursor click; staggered text arrivals.
Music: bed at full presence.
Transition mood: slide → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Flow: questionnaire to booked call — 7s

On screen: the working app simulated — step cards "01 Basic Info" through "05 Logistics" arriving one by one, a file chip ("specs.pdf") attaching, a calendar date highlighting, then a "Discovery Received!" confirmation card stamping in.
Sequential/interaction: yes — the centerpiece: 5 step cards in sequence, file attach, date pick, confirmation stamp. Full set holds ~1.5s at the end.
Audio intent: momentum and competence; card pops, a stamp thud on confirmation.
Audio-coupled idea: card-by-card sequence; stamp thud.
Music: bed drives the sequence.
Transition mood: clean wipe → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Pricing punchline + logo — 5s

On screen: three glass pricing cards — "10,000 / 25,000 / 45,000 EGP" — popping in left to right, then the line "Fixed scope. No hidden fees." Cut to ShenoDev logo + "shenodev.tech".
Sequential/interaction: yes — price cards pop in sequence, then hold with the punchline (~1.5s settled for the 5-word line).
Audio intent: payoff; dry logo hit on the final frame.
Audio-coupled idea: card pops; final hit.
Music: final hit, quick fade.
Transition mood: end.
