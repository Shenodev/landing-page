# Brag Plan: ShenoDev (Vertical Reel)

## What is this app?

ShenoDev (shenodev.tech) is a premium full-stack web development agency site with a working quote machine: a 5-step project discovery questionnaire, Calendly call scheduling, and confirmation emails — plus transparent fixed EGP pricing.

## The angle

Same precision-launch-film angle as the landscape cut, reframed for the phone screen: the tagline stacked huge across two lines, the flow shown as a thumb-scroll of step cards, prices stacked like a rate card. "Think it, Sheno it." — delivered straight, proven in 20 seconds.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)

"Think it," / "Sheno it." stacked in giant Sora type filling the 9:16 frame over a cyan glow pulse. Two-line stack reads instantly on a phone.

## Key moments (the middle)

- The hero headline rebuilt stacked for vertical, with a tap on the full-width "Start Your Project" button.
- The discovery flow as a vertical card stack: 01→05 arriving top to bottom like a scroll, file chip + date pick, then the "Discovery Received!" stamp.
- The three price cards (10,000 / 25,000 / 45,000 EGP) stacked full-width under "Fixed scope. No hidden fees."

## Outro / punchline

"Fixed scope. No hidden fees." holds, then the ShenoDev logo + shenodev.tech. Same anti-punchline: bragging about *not* surprising you.

## User flow worth showing

Start Your Project → 5-step discovery questionnaire → Calendly scheduling → "Thank you — Discovery Received!" confirmation. Centerpiece scene simulates it as a vertical stack.

## Tone

- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: premium agency reel — vertical cut of the launch film
- Interpretation: stacked layouts, larger type for phone viewing, same restraint; sequential reveals slightly punchier to suit reel pacing, holds still honored

## Format: vertical — 1080x1920
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)

- Background: #0b1326 (deep slate)
- Accent: #06B6D4 (electric cyan; bright variant #4cd7f6)
- Text: #dae2fd (off-white; pure highlights #F8FAFC)
- Display font: Sora
- Body font: Inter
- Strongest visual element: glassmorphism cards with cyan glow, stacked full-width for the phone frame

## Share copy (draft)

Think it, Sheno it. ⚡ Fixed-scope web apps, no hidden fees — shenodev.tech 📱 Full breakdown in the reel.

## Audio direction

- Role: sleek modern bed with motion-matched accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (60s, ~110 BPM, ende.app)
- Music treatment: fade in under the hook (0–2s), full presence through flow/pricing, hard hit + quick fade on the logo outro
- Music cue guidance: preset `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.*` read in the landscape run; ~110 BPM (~0.55s spacing). Strong cues near 18.01s / 18.55s for the logo hit; boundaries near ~5.2s, ~10.4s, ~15.8s may snap within ±0.15s. Card reveals on every *other* beat at most, then hold.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle — cyan glow breathing with music energy in hook/outro only
- SFX posture: sparse, professional — whooshes, card pops, tap, stamp thud, one dry logo hit
- Audio-coupled moments: hook slam + glow pulse; tap on "Start Your Project"; step cards arriving top to bottom; price cards stacking; confirmation stamp thud
- Restraint rule: music must never fight readability; no SFX under held reading text
